# Political biography and movement excerpts

The authors of the linked Chinese, English and Spanish Wikipedia article revisions contributed the prose displayed in the leader and political movement tooltips. Each tooltip title links to its source revision. The history of that article identifies its contributors. These excerpts and their Chinese translations are made available under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The presentation selects complete sentences, groups them into passages, simplifies Chinese characters and translates selected foreign-language passages into Chinese. For translated entries the 原文 control displays the selected foreign-language passages. The revision links, exact source passages for translated records and displayed versions are distributed in political-excerpts.js. Wikimedia does not endorse this project.

Portraits and protest photographs have separate [media credits](politics/assets/figures/WORLD-SOURCES.md) and [original credits](politics/assets/figures/SOURCES.md).
