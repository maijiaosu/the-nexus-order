# China/Russia Domestic-Affairs Study Sources and Assets

Checked: 2026-09-26. This directory contains standalone studies only. The production program, production data, dist, and Obsidian plugin were not changed.

## Content Sources

### China

- [National People's Congress: Constitution of the People's Republic of China (2018)](https://www.npc.gov.cn/zgrdw/npc/xinwen/2018-03/22/content_2052489.htm)：Statutory functions of the NPC, President, State Council, Central Military Commission, judiciary, and supervisory institutions. The UI relationship diagram provides thematic navigation, not a complete constitutional hierarchy.
- [Ministry of Education reproduction of the Fifteenth Five-Year Plan outline](https://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xwfb/xw_zt/moe_357/2026/2026_zt03/yw/202603/t20260314_1430877.html)：The 2026–2030 plan, including annual R&D-spending growth above 7%. UI targets are labeled as targets rather than misrepresented as achieved values.
- [Cyberspace Administration: Fifteenth Five-Year Plan digital-economy content](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2026-04/01/c_1776782392823569.htm)：The target for core digital-economy industries to account for 12.5% of GDP in 2030.
- [Growth target in the 2026 Government Work Report](https://jsca.miit.gov.cn/xwdt/szyw/art/2026/art_e3831839c17e42dd82ca61fb477f9018.html)：2026 GDP-growth target of 4.5%–5%.
- [National Bureau of Statistics: January–August 2026 fixed-asset investment](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202609/t20260915_1965309.html)：Fixed-asset investment down 7.2% year on year.[Questions and answers from the same period](https://www.stats.gov.cn/zwfwck/sjfb/202609/t20260915_1965332.html)。
- [IMF 2025 China Article IV consultation report, released in 2026](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/cr/issues/2026/02/17/peoples-republic-of-china-2025-article-iv-consultation-press-release-staff-report-and-574028)：Property adjustment, local finances, domestic demand, and population aging. UI social-pressure content is a summary and supplies no invented stability score.
- [AP Taiwan Strait military-exercise report, 2026-09-18](https://apnews.com/article/e7c1c16040f6f9ca5e7672a3fda0ab60)：Taiwan's de facto self-government, Beijing's position, military context, and US arms sales. UI statements distinguish Beijing's claims from Taipei's position rather than treating either side's assertions as uncontested fact.
- [Reuters Taiwan chip diplomacy, 2026-09-07](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/taiwan-flexes-chip-diplomacy-muscles-as-it-faces-pressure-to-share-ai-wealth-with-allies-4890346?ampMode=1)：Semiconductor industry and links with external partners.

Ideological categories and trend arrows retain the CHN.pie semantics in the project's root data.js: authoritarian-socialist right, left, and center; Maoist New Left; democratic-socialist broad left; liberal opposition; populism; and ultranationalism. These are categories from the user's existing project, not opinion polling obtained during this research. The original pie chart remains in the production program's outer sidebar. This study did not modify, move, or copy it, nor create another proportional chart inside the page. Text conceptually explains labels rather than asserting shares of China's population.

### Russia

- [Russian Presidency: presidential powers](https://kremlin.ru/structure/president)：Powers including determination of fundamental policy directions.
- [Russian Presidency: Security Council](https://en.kremlin.ru/structure/security-council/members)：Presidential chairmanship and membership.
- [Bank of Russia press office decision, 2026-09-11](https://www.cbr.ru/eng/press/keypr)：Key rate 14%; annual inflation of 6.3% on September 7; labor-market and productive-capacity constraints. The URL is a rolling page, but the study's values are fixed to those dates.
- [Bank of Russia analyst survey](https://cbr.ru/eng/statistics/ddkp/mo_br/?trk=public_post_comment-text)：Survey conducted in late August–early September 2026: median growth forecasts of 0.5% for 2026 and 1.2% for 2027. The UI explicitly identifies them as forecasts.
- [Council of the EU: ending Russian energy imports](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/ending-russian-energy-imports/)及[Timeline](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/ending-russian-energy-imports/timeline-ending-russian-energy-imports/)：Phaseout arrangements and exceptions for long-term LNG contracts in January 2027 and pipeline gas in September 2027.
- [AP: Power of Siberia 2, 2025-09-10](https://apnews.com/article/b48dffa3b9527cbccfa7585a03ca3c17)：Project intentions, pricing, and construction issues. The project is not claimed to be operational.
- [Reuters, reproduced: eastbound gas and replacement of European markets, 2025-10-07](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2025/10/07/russias-power-of-siberia-2-deal-with-china-would-take-a-decade-to-boost-gas-exports-reuters-a90744)：Construction lead times and bargaining constraints.
- [AP Russian economic pressure, 2026-09-18](https://apnews.com/article/79bf1c1abd5da48769ed8dab4a766de0)、[Le Monde 2026-09-15](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/economy/article/2026/09/15/russian-economy-increasingly-fragile-after-ukraine-drone-attacks_6757546_19.html)：Context of pressure from war and on civilian sectors. The UI does not describe Russia as facing imminent total collapse.

The diplomatic page is an editorial summary of relationship structures. It adds no unverified latest negotiation results, precise front lines, or live polling. The Russian parliament does not reuse old 2021 seat counts as 2026 seats.
